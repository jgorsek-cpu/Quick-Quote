What the parser looks for

Labelled fields — customer, brand, formula, SKU, dosage form, capsule size and type, serving size, servings and count per bottle, volume, MOQ, timeline, claims, testing.

An ingredient table — a header row naming both an ingredient column and an amount column. Amounts in mg, mcg or g are converted; IU is left unknown rather than guessed.

Packaging — a component table, or Bottle: 175cc white HDPE style lines.
